## 1. Abstract
The Blood Donor Emergency Network is a Flutter-based prototype intended to help users organise potential blood donor details and record emergency blood requests. The project promotes awareness of timely blood donation and aligns with SDG 3: Good Health and Well-being.

## 2. Problem statement
During urgent situations, finding a potential donor with a suitable blood group in a nearby city can be difficult. This prototype demonstrates a simple interface for searching sample donors and recording requests.

## 3. Objectives
- Provide a simple and accessible mobile interface.
- Register potential donors using a basic form.
- Search sample donor entries by blood group and city.
- Record and display emergency blood requests.
- Promote awareness of SDG 3.

## 4. SDG relevance
**SDG 3 — Good Health and Well-being.** The project supports awareness and community coordination. It does not replace hospitals, blood banks, clinical screening, or emergency services.

## 9. Limitations and future scope
The prototype has no secure login, persistent/cloud database, real-time notifications, map, or verified donor eligibility workflow. Future work could add Firebase or another secure backend, authentication and role-based access, opt-in contact sharing, privacy controls, donor verification workflows, and notifications after safety and privacy review.

## 10. Conclusion
The prototype demonstrates how a mobile application can organise sample donor information and emergency blood requests while promoting SDG 3. It requires a secure backend and appropriate validation before any real-world deployment.
